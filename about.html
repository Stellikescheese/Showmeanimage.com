"I'm super cool and you should give me all your money" — Me

I'm Stel, a young and aspiring Melbourne/Naarm based photographer looking to make myself known.

My work spans people, cars, product, street, architecture, and concert photography. Every moment captured with knowledge and creative intent.

Shooting both film and digital, I blend the character of analogue with the precision of modern capture.

Having had a camera in my hands for over a decade and have over three years of professional experience, I am confident I can bring you a great experience and high quality images.
