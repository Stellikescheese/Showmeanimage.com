My name is Stel, I am a melbourne based photographer drawn to stories in everyday moments, captured and communicated with care and consideration.

My work spans a wide range of subjects including: Concerts, People, Cars, and Architecture. I approach every shoot with passion and enthusiasm, always excited to see what I can capture.

I shoot with both film and digital. My style comes from a blend of analogue character with modern technological precision.
